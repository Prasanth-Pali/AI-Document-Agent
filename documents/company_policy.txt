# Company Leave Policy

Employees can apply for annual leave through the company's HR portal.

Employees should submit leave requests at least five working days before the planned leave date.

The manager must approve the leave request before the employee takes leave.

For sick leave, employees should notify their manager as soon as possible.

Employees can check their remaining leave balance through the HR portal.

# IT Support Policy

Employees who experience application access issues should raise a ticket with the IT support team.

Every IT support ticket must include the application name, a description of the issue, and any relevant error message.

Employees should never share their passwords or API keys in support tickets.

# Work From Home Policy

Employees must follow their department's work-from-home approval process.

Employees should use company-approved devices and secure network connections when accessing company systems remotely.
